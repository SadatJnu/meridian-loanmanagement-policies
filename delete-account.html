Delete your account — Meridian Loan Management

App: Meridian Loan Management (Google Play, package com.meridian.loan_management)

Google Play developer: RoyCraft. RoyCraft publishes this app on Google Play on behalf of Meridian Softworks, which is responsible for the app and for the data described on this page.

This page explains how to delete your Meridian Loan Management account and the data linked to it. It is the web resource required by Google Play. *Publish it at a public URL and put that URL into Play Console (Data safety > Data deletion). Until it is published, the URL is pending.*

Option 1 — in the app (recommended)

  • Sign in and open Settings.
  • Tap Delete my account.
  • Read what is deleted and kept, enter your password, type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

Deleted at once on the phone: your name, sign-in name, email address, password, profile photo, remembered login and registration reports; the account is disabled and you are signed out. If your name and email had been sent to Meridian Softworks, the app sends a request to delete them; it is completed when our service confirms it (an Owner sees the status in Settings > Data deletion requests).

Option 2 — without the app

Email meridiansoftworksltd@gmail.com with the subject Delete my data, from the address you registered with (or write the name and email address to delete). We delete the registration record within 30 days and confirm by email.
