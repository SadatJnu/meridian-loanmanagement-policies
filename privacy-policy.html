Privacy Policy — Meridian Loan Management

Publisher: Meridian Softworks · Contact: meridiansoftworksltd@gmail.com App: Meridian Loan Management (Android, package com.meridian.loan_management) Last updated: 22 November 2026

Google Play developer: RoyCraft. RoyCraft publishes this app on Google Play on behalf of Meridian Softworks, which is responsible for the app and for the data described on this page.

Summary

Meridian Loan Management is an offline record book for money you lend and borrow. Your records stay on your phone in an encrypted database. The app does not give loans, does not process payments and does not show ads. The only information sent to us is the name and email address of a person registered in the app, so that we know who uses the app and can send one welcome email.

1. Information stored only on your phone

Everything you enter — organization details, users, customers and their identity details, loans, repayments, personal borrowing and lending records, profile photos, reports and settings — is stored only on your device in an encrypted (SQLCipher) database. We cannot see it. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

2. Information we receive

When a user is registered with an email address (first-time setup or "Add user"), and the phone is online, the app sends to Meridian Softworks over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection:

  • the person's name and email address,
  • the app version, the time of registration and a random registration number.

We use it to know how many people use the app and to send one welcome email to that address. It is kept in a private Google Sheet of the Meridian Softworks Google account; the sending is done by a Google Apps Script service that we operate. We do not sell or share this information with anyone and do not use it for advertising. Passwords, PINs, backup passphrases, customer data and financial records are never sent.

3. Google services on your phone

  • "Use my Google account" (optional): Android's Google account chooser gives the app the email address of the account you pick, only to fill the email field. Nothing else is sent to us.
  • Automatic backup (optional): the app writes an encrypted backup file into a folder you choose (for example in your own Google Drive). The file is encrypted with your passphrase before it leaves the app; we have no access to it. Google Drive's own privacy policy applies to your Drive.

4. Permissions

Internet (registration report and welcome email), biometric unlock (optional), background work (automatic backup), network state. Profile photos use Android's photo picker or camera app and are stored only on your phone.

5. Children

The app is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed to children.

6. Security

Local data is encrypted; data sent to us travels over HTTPS; backups are encrypted with a passphrase only you know. No method is 100% secure, but we use standard protection and do not keep your financial data.

7. Deleting your data

In the app (each user, their own account): Settings > Delete my account. After you enter your password and type DELETE, the app at once deletes from the phone your name, sign-in name, email address, password, profile photo, remembered login and registration reports, disables the account for good, and — if your name and email had been sent to us — sends us a request to delete them. That request is shown as completed only after our service confirms the deletion; an Owner can see its status in Settings > Data deletion requests.

What is kept: the organization's financial records you entered or approved (loans, receipts, ledger, personal borrowing and lending) and the audit log stay on the organization's phone, because they belong to the organization and are append-only; they then show "Deleted user". We never hold these records.

Deleting all of an organization's data: the Owner makes a backup if needed and uninstalls the app, which removes everything it stored on the phone. Backup files remain in the folder you chose until you delete them.

Without the app (web request): email meridiansoftworksltd@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" from the registered address, or give the name and email to delete. We delete the registration record within 30 days and confirm by email. More detail: the "Delete your account" page of Meridian Loan Management.

8. Changes

We will update this page if the app starts to handle information differently and change the "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Meridian Softworks — meridiansoftworksltd@gmail.com
